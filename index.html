    A day like this, roughly

    No two days are identical, but this is close to a typical shift.

    1. 8:00 AM

      Inbox & ticket triage

      Sort overnight messages across email, chat, and social DMs, and flag anything urgent first.

    2. 10:00 AM

      Customer support queue

      Work through tickets and calls, aiming to resolve on first contact and spot upsell opportunities along the way.

    3. 1:00 PM

      Content & social

      Draft captions, schedule posts, and check how yesterday's content performed.

    4. 3:00 PM

      Project check-ins

      Update task boards, follow up with the team, and keep client projects moving on schedule.

    5. 5:00 PM

      Wrap-up & reporting

      Log what got done, prep tomorrow's priorities, and send a short end-of-day update.
